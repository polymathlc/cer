Selection:

Erase / Paint: drag over the image — click then Shift-click joins the two points with a straight line (Photoshop style), and Shift-drag locks the stroke to horizontal / vertical. Fill: click inside a shape to flood it with colour. Clone: click to set the source, then drag to stamp that area elsewhere (Alt+click re-sets the source). History: brush the original picture back by hand. Select / Lasso / Wand: mark out an area, then fill it, move it, or use AI content-aware fill (Esc deselects). Move: drag inside a selection to cut it out and drop it anywhere (Alt to copy), or drag a text label to reposition it. Line: drag from start to end. Text: click, type, then Enter.
Scroll over the image to zoom (down to single pixels); hold Space (or the middle mouse button) and drag to pan; scroll over either slider to change it. Set the brush to 1 px for pixel-perfect edits.

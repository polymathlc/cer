# Tutor companions for worksheets

These are the original Study Buddy character assets from `polymathlc/tutor`,
copied unchanged from `assets/companions/` for CER's worksheet builders.

- **Orbit:** teal and cream robot with a golden antenna and chest light.
- **Pip:** orange fox with a cream muzzle and teal neckerchief.
- **Nova:** plum owl with a cream face and golden glasses.

Each companion has `welcome`, `thinking`, `encourage`, and `celebrate` poses.
All twelve files are transparent 512 × 512 WebP images. The Tutor repository's
`docs/character-art.md` records their original generation on 2026-09-23 and
validation; no external source artwork was used. The character identities and
poses have not been redrawn for CER.

`worksheet-art.js` owns the catalogue, safe rendering, and print styles. Its
colourful science illustrations are original self-contained inline SVG drawings
created for these worksheet builders; they do not need a network image service.

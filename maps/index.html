Questions

How do I cancel?

Sign in on this page and tap Cancel subscription. You keep the library until the end of the month you've paid for, and you're never charged again. Changed your mind? Tap Keep my subscription before that date.

I'm a Polymath student. Do I pay?

No. Sign in with the account you use for My Polymath. Once the centre has approved it, every map opens free. You can also find the maps inside the Mind Map app.

What if a payment doesn't go through?

The library pauses until the card is fixed. Tap Update card on this page, or WhatsApp us on +65 9645 0588 and we'll help.

Which levels are the maps for?

Primary Science and Math, mostly P3 to P6. Each map shows its topics on the card, so you can see what's inside before you subscribe.

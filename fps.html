Infinite dungeon. Infinite loot. Every 15 seconds, science decides your luck.

← Back to Science Quest

🧬 Permanent upgrades ⚛ 0 cores

Earn Science Cores every run (floors, kills, correct answers). Upgrades persist forever — that's the "lite" in roguelite.

Controls: WASD move · mouse look (left/right AND up/down) · click shoot · R reload · E pick up loot · Esc pause
Crosshair blooms while you move and fire — stand still for accurate shots.
Headshots: every enemy has a small critical zone (the head) — hit it for ×2 damage. Enemies are tanky, so crits are the fast way to drop them. Aim too high or into the floor and the shot misses!
Loot rarities: Common · Uncommon · Rare · Epic · Legendary · Mythic (unique special effects!)
Elements: 🔥 burn · ❄ slow · ⚡ stun · ☠ poison
Questions: one every 15s. Streaks raise loot luck; milestones (3/5/8/12) guarantee better drops. A wrong answer resets the streak.
Clear every enemy on a floor to open the portal to the next one. Dungeons never end — how deep can you go?

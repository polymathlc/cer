🔫 Science StrikeBETA

Open world. Infinite waves. Every 15 seconds, science decides your luck.

← Back to Science Quest

🧬 Permanent upgrades ⚛ 0 cores

Earn Science Cores every run (floors, kills, correct answers). Upgrades persist forever — that's the "lite" in roguelite.

Controls: WASD move · mouse look (left/right AND up/down) · click shoot · right-click aim/scope · R reload · E pick up loot · Q grenade · F class ability · I skill tree · Esc pause
Classes: 🪖 Soldier (assault rifle, Bullet Time) · 🎯 Sniper (huge range, slow heavy shots, sees crit boxes, traps) · 🔧 Engineer (turrets, healing drones). Each class has a 100-node skill tree with prerequisites — cores are banked and saved the instant you earn them, so you can stop any time and lose nothing.
Bullets are real: every shot streaks out of your muzzle as a glowing tracer and flies until it hits a monster, a tree trunk, or runs out of range. Enemies standing beside trees CAN be shot — only the trunk itself blocks bullets.
Crosshair blooms while you move and fire — stand still (or scope in) for accurate shots.
Headshots: all 25 monster types have their own small critical zone where their head ACTUALLY is (a beetle's head is low, a mech's is high) — hit it for ×2 damage. Enemies are tanky, so crits are the fast way to drop them. Aim too high or into the floor and the shot misses!
Loot rarities: Common · Uncommon · Rare · Epic · Legendary · Mythic (unique special effects!)
Elements: 🔥 burn · ❄ slow · ⚡ stun · ☠ poison
Questions: one every 15s. Streaks raise loot luck; milestones (3/5/8/12) guarantee better drops. A wrong answer resets the streak.
Minimap (top-left): red blips are enemies — far ones stick to the rim so you always know which way to turn. Grey lines are roads, the sun sits at a fixed compass point.
Wipe a wave to get a 5-second breather, then a bigger wave rolls in. Waves never end — how long can you last?

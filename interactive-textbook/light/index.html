What are the properties of light that explain why a shadow is formed?

There are two properties of light that can explain why a shadow is formed:

  • Light travels in straight lines.
  • Light cannot pass through an opaque object. Only some light can pass through a translucent object.

What factors affect the size and shape of shadows formed?

When you are outdoors, you may have noticed that the size and shape of the shadow of a tree formed are different at different times of the day. Do you know why?

Let us look at the factors that affect the size and shape of shadows.

What affects the size of shadows?

Shadows of different sizes can be formed by the same object. The size of a shadow depends on

  • the distance between the object and the source of light, and
  • the distance between the object and the surface on which the shadow is formed.

The surface on which a shadow is formed is called the screen.

How does the distance between the object and the source of light affect the size of the shadow?

As the source of light is placed further away from the object, the size of the shadow decreases. As the source of light is placed closer to the object, the size of the shadow increases.

Part 9 · Textbook p. 108

Our shadows through the day

🌏 Science Around Us

When we are outdoors on a clear, sunny day, we see our shadows on the ground. This is because our bodies block sunlight from reaching the ground.

Shadow formed on the ground

Have you noticed that the size, the shape and the position of your shadow change at different times of the day?

Early in the morning and late in the afternoon, the Sun is at its lowest position in the sky. Therefore, our shadows look very long. Nearer to noon, our shadows are the shortest, as the Sun is directly above us.

The shadow formed in the morning points in the opposite direction from the shadow formed in the evening. This is because the Sun rises in the East in the morning and sets in the West in the evening.

As the shadow changes in position and length throughout the day, people in ancient times used the changing shadow to tell time. They created a sundial, which showed the changing shadow of a rod at different times of the day.

Sundial

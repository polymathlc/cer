What causes temperature to change?

MumBelle, the cold milk is now warm. You may drink it.

BelleThank you, Mum. I wonder why the temperature of the cold milk increased when the bottle was put in the basin of hot water.

When you place a bottle of cold milk into a basin of hot water, you will find that the hot water becomes less hot while the cold milk becomes less cold after some time. We say that the temperature of the hot water decreases and the temperature of the cold milk increases.

Have you ever wondered what causes the temperature of the cold milk to increase and the temperature of the hot water to decrease when the two are in contact with each other?

Scientists explain that the temperature of an object changes because of heat. What is heat? Like light, heat is a form of energy. Heat can cause changes in temperature. When an object gains heat, its temperature increases. When an object loses heat, its temperature decreases.

How does heat flow?

In the previous chapter, you learnt that when a bottle of cold milk is placed in a basin of hot water, the temperature of the cold milk increases while the temperature of the hot water decreases.

💻 Let's Inquire
  • AB: Activity 7.1 (Part A)
  • SLS: Heat Flow

The changes in temperature can be explained by the heat flow from the hot water to the cold milk. In fact, heat always flows from an object with a higher temperature to an object with a lower temperature. We can use this concept of heat to explain many of the changes in temperature happening around us.

ExampleObservation after some timeExplanation of observation
A pot of water placed on the stove

The metal pot and the water become hotter.

The flame on the stove has a higher temperature than the metal pot and the water.

Therefore, heat will flow from the flame to the pot of water.

Since the water in the pot gains heat, its temperature increases.
A cold metal spoon placed in a cup of hot coffee

The metal spoon feels hot.

The coffee has a higher temperature than the metal spoon.

Therefore, heat will flow from the hot coffee to the cold metal spoon.

Since the metal spoon gains heat, its temperature increases.
A cup of hot tea left on a table

The hot tea cools down.

The hot tea has a higher temperature than its surroundings.

Therefore, heat will flow from the hot tea to its surroundings.

Since the hot tea loses heat, its temperature decreases. Similarly, since the table and the air around the tea gain heat, their temperatures increase.

What is a source of heat?

You have learnt that heat will always flow from an object with a higher temperature to an object with a lower temperature. An object that has a higher temperature than its surroundings is a source of heat.

A pot of water placed on a stove

Since the flame on the stove has a higher temperature than the pot of water, heat will flow from the flame to the water in the pot. Therefore, the flame is the source of heat.

A cold metal spoon placed in a cup of hot coffee

Since the hot coffee has a higher temperature than the cold metal spoon, heat will flow from the hot coffee to the cold metal spoon. Therefore, the hot coffee is the source of heat.

A cup of hot tea left on a table

Since the hot tea has a higher temperature than the table and the air surrounding it, heat will flow from the hot tea to its surroundings. Therefore, the hot tea is the source of heat.

Materials that allow heat to flow through them quickly are called good conductors of heat. Examples of good conductors of heat are metals.

Materials that do not allow heat to flow through them quickly are called poor conductors of heat. Examples of poor conductors of heat are glass, plastic and wood.

Examples of good conductors and poor conductors of heat

While good conductors of heat are very useful in our daily life, they can also cause problems at times. Imagine if the handles of metal pots or electric irons were made of metal. They would be too hot for us to hold once they are heated.

🌏 Science Around Us

Expansion and contraction of objects due to heat can be useful in our daily life. If you have a metal jar lid that is too tight to open, you simply need to heat up the lid using some hot water. The expansion of the metal lid will make it easier to open the lid.

However, expansion and contraction of objects due to heat can also cause problems in our daily life. Repeated expansion and contraction of a road can cause it to crack if there is no space for it to expand. To solve the problem, engineers have created gaps in the road to allow for expansion of the road on hot days.

What I Have Learnt

Chapter 6 · Heat

  1. Temperature is a measure of how hot or cold something is.
  2. Temperature can be measured using a thermometer in degree Celsius (°C).
  3. Heat is a form of energy. It causes temperature to change. When an object loses heat, its temperature decreases. When an object gains heat, its temperature increases.
  4. Temperature is not the same as heat. Temperature is a property of an object, while heat gained or lost is the reason why the temperature of an object changes.

Chapter 7 · Effects of Heat

  1. Heat flows from an object or an area with a higher temperature to an object or an area with a lower temperature until they have the same temperature.
  2. An object that has a higher temperature than its surroundings is a source of heat.
  3. Materials that allow heat to flow through them quickly are called good conductors of heat. Materials that do not allow heat to flow through them quickly are called poor conductors of heat.
  4. Heat can cause:
    a. a change in temperature.
    b. objects to expand or contract. When an object gains heat, it will expand. When an object loses heat, it will contract.
    c. a change in the state of matter.

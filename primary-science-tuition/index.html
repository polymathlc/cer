Why children lose marks in Science, and how we fix it.

Most children who struggle in Primary Science know more than their marks show. They lose marks in the open-ended questions: an answer that is true but does not use the data, a keyword that is missing, or an explanation that stops halfway. Our lessons teach the reasoning and the answering skills, not just the facts.

ANALYSE

Read the question like a scientist

Children learn to spot what changed, what was measured and what the question is really asking before they write a word.

EXPLAIN

Claim, Evidence, Reasoning

Our proprietary TCQ-CER approach gives every answer a clear Claim, Evidence taken from the question, and scientific Reasoning that links them.

PRACTISE

Practice that adapts

Between lessons, Polymath Online picks questions by level and progress, marks open-ended answers with AI feedback, and turns mistakes into revision cards.

What we cover at each level.

Lessons follow the Singapore primary Science syllabus and its five themes: Diversity, Cycles, Systems, Interactions and Energy. We build each idea from the foundations up to PSLE-level questions.

P3 · P4

Strong foundations

  • Living and non-living things, plants, animals and fungi
  • Materials and their properties, magnets
  • Life cycles, matter and its states
  • Plant and human systems
  • Light, shadows and heat
P5

The big ideas

  • Reproduction in plants and humans
  • Water and the water cycle
  • Plant transport, respiratory and circulatory systems
  • Cells and electrical systems
P6 · PSLE

Putting it all together

  • Forces, energy forms and energy conversion
  • Photosynthesis and food chains and webs
  • Organisms and their environment
  • Full PSLE papers, timing and exam technique

Questions parents often ask.

Which levels do you teach for Science?

Primary 3 to Primary 6, with support up to Secondary 1. Classes are grouped by level so every lesson matches what your child is learning in school.

How do you prepare students for PSLE Science?

We teach the concepts for every syllabus theme, then train the answering skills that open-ended questions need: reading the question, using the data given, and explaining with the right keywords using our TCQ-CER approach. P6 students also practise full PSLE papers.

Where are the classes?

In person at our centre in Clementi, Singapore, and live online. Both follow the same curriculum with the same teacher, so families can choose what suits them.

Can my child try a lesson first?

Yes. We offer a paid trial lesson: a full 1 hour 45 minute class with the group your child would join. You can see the timetable and book a trial on the schedule page.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the level and the class. WhatsApp us on +65 9645 0588 or send an enquiry and we will reply with the current fees and timetable for your child’s level.

What is included besides the lesson?

Every enrolled student gets Polymath Online at no extra cost: guided practice, AI marking and feedback, revision cards, PSLE past papers, progress reports and Science Quest.

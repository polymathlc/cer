Your first day, step by step

  1. Check your invitation email. Your teacher adds your Google account to your class. The email links you to the Online portal.
  2. Sign in with Google. Use the same account on every app. Centre students can also be signed in by the teacher using Practise as in Study Buddy.
  3. Find your class. Online and Ans Key show the classes that match your level (P3 to P6) and subject.
  4. Open the current worksheet. It is highlighted on your class timeline. View it, print it, or practise it in Ans Key.
  5. Claim your name in Rewards. Pick your level, time slot and name, then press That's me so your marks are counted.
🔒 Privacy tip: never share your Google password with friends. If something looks wrong with your account, tell your teacher in class.

🧭 Key takeaway

  • One Google account opens all five apps.
  • Online is where worksheets appear first; Ans Key is where you check them.
  • Rewards only counts your marks after you claim your name.
Chapter 2

The Science Learning Portal (CER)

CER stands for Claim, Evidence and Reasoning. It is the way strong Science answers are built.

In PSLE open-ended questions, marks go to answers that say what happens, point to what the question shows, and explain the science behind it. The portal trains exactly that. Every practice card asks you three things:

🅲 Claim

What do you conclude?

🅴 Evidence

What in the question supports it?

🆁 Reasoning

Explain the science linking them.

When you press Check my answer, the AI marks it as correct, partly correct or incorrect. It then shows the model answer and a Picture it diagram so you can see the idea, and lets you choose Try this question again or Next question.

Try it yourself

Preview the CER questions

These four questions work just like the ones in the portal. Start with a multiple-choice warm-up, then build a full CER answer, fill in the keywords, and finally hunt for a weak answer.

⚡ Warm-up · Multiple choice
Energy · Heat

Question 1Two ice cubes of the same size are placed on a metal tray and a wooden tray at room temperature. Why does the ice on the metal tray melt faster?

Notice option D. "Cold escaping" is one of the most common Science mistakes. Heat is what moves, and it always flows from the warmer place to the cooler place. Keep that in mind for the next question.

🧩 Build a CER answer

Question 2Cup A is wrapped in felt. Cup B is uncovered. Both begin with water at 80°C. Which cup keeps the water hot for longer? Explain your answer.

Pick one card from each column. Your answer builds itself underneath.

A full-mark answer needs all three parts. A claim on its own ("Cup A") usually earns nothing, because the marker cannot see your thinking. The reasoning must name the concept, here poor conductor of heat, which is why keywords matter so much.

P4 · Diversity

Question 4A fruit has hooks on its surface. A student was asked how this helps the plant. Which of these answers would lose marks?

🔬 Key takeaway

  • Always give a Claim, the Evidence from the question, and the Reasoning (the science).
  • Heat moves, cold does not. It flows from warmer to cooler.
  • Use the exact keywords: conductor, evaporate, water vapour, dispersal.
  • Check that your answer fits the question being asked.

How the mistake loop works

  1. Answer a question in Quick or Topical Practice.
  2. Read the verdict. A partly correct answer names the Sidekick you missed.
  3. Open Learn from Mistakes and pick the mistake animal for that answer.
  4. Rewrite it. The improved answer is marked again.
  5. Review Revision Cards. Mistakes you repeat become flashcards so they stick.

    Chapter 4

    Study Buddy

    Study Buddy is an AI tutor called Chung GPT. It gives you hints, not answers, so the thinking stays yours.

    Family students sign in with Google. At the centre, your teacher can sign you in with Practise as. A session lasts 4 hours. For P3, Study Buddy covers Science only.

    How a session works

    1. Open a worksheet. Pick a PDF, or one marked 📌 Set for you by your teacher.
    2. Write on it. Use the pen, highlighter, text, shapes and eraser right on the page.
    3. Stuck? Climb the 💡 help ladder. Each rung gives a little more help.
    4. 💬 Ask your buddy anything about the question.
    5. ✅ Mark my work. You get ✅ correct, 🟡 almost or ❌ not quite.
    6. 📕 Mistake book. Wrong answers are saved so you can come back to them.

    Try the help ladder on a real Math question. Unlock one rung at a time and notice how the answer only comes at the very end.

    Help

    Frequently asked questions

    I signed in but I cannot see my class.
    Check that you used the Google account your teacher invited. If it still does not appear, tell your teacher so they can add that account to the class.
    Why can I not see the name of a later worksheet?
    Worksheets are released up to five ahead. Later ones are counted on your timeline, but their names stay hidden until your teacher releases them.
    Will Study Buddy just give me the answer?
    Only at the last rung of the help ladder. It starts with a nudge, then the concept, then the method. Most students solve it before the last rung.
    Do hints cost me XP or points?
    No. Hints never cost points in Study Buddy.
    How do I get game credits in CER?
    Answer questions. Every 5 questions gives you 1 🎟️ credit, and each Arcade game costs 1 credit. Pirate Rift and Crew Defense are free.
    Are my Hero XP and Rewards marks the same thing?
    No. Hero XP lives in Ans Key and grows your hero. Rewards marks are counted in the Rewards app and can be spent on prizes.
    Can my parents see my work?
    Yes. Parents see the same Online timeline you do, and it updates live.
    Is my camera ever turned on without asking?
    No. Live cams only share your camera when you choose Share my camera.
    What file can I upload for test paper marks?
    A PDF up to 10MB. Each upload earns +10 marks.

Questions parents often ask.

Are online classes live or recorded?

Live. Your child joins the class in real time with Mr Chung and can ask questions during the lesson, just like at the centre.

Is the online class the same as the centre class?

Yes. Online classes follow the same curriculum, the same TCQ-CER and Heuristics frameworks and the same teacher as our Clementi centre.

How is homework marked online?

Work is submitted through Polymath Online and marked with feedback. Children can also upload a photo of handwritten work with Snap & Mark.

Can my child try a lesson first?

Yes. We offer a paid trial lesson: a full 1 hour 45 minute class with the group your child would join. You can see the timetable and book a trial on the schedule page.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the level and the class. WhatsApp us on +65 9645 0588 or send an enquiry and we will reply with the current fees and timetable for your child’s level.

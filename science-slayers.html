🗡️ Science Slayers

Descend a procedurally generated dungeon. Monster packs idle in their lairs until you get close — pick your fights, hunt magic packs and legendary uniques, slay the floor boss for loot, and take the stairs down to harder floors with harder questions.

🧪 Beta build — no Science Slayers leaderboard yet. Questions you answer here still count toward the main leaderboard and your monthly questions total. Your Armory gear and skill loadouts are shared with Science Legends.
  • Move with WASD / arrows, or click the ground to walk there. Click an enemy to attack it, hold right-click to fire at the crosshair, and skills 1–4 fire at the crosshair.
  • Each class knows 12 skills — unlock, rank, branch and crown them with 1-of-3 ULTIMATES in a huge skill tree (T), then pick your 4 with 🎯 Select Skill (K).
  • Everything spends action points: basic attacks 1 ⚡, skills 4 ⚡, and even the auto-attacks from unique/mythic gear 2–3 ⚡. At zero, a science MCQ appears — correct = full refill +1 skill point; wrong = only ⅓ of your actions next turn.
  • Each floor hides a boss — kill it for a guaranteed item drop (0.5% Mythic!) and the stairs to the next depth.
  • Spend app points on loot boxes in the Armory (B) — permanent helm, armour, gloves, greaves, rings & amulet. Unique and Mythic gear carries skill-changing powers.

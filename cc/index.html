How the quest board works
  1. Dad adds a quest for Caius, Caris or both, with the Robux it is worth.
  2. Finish the quest, then tell Dad. He marks it Success and the Robux go into your balance.
  3. If a quest is not done in time, Dad may mark it Failed. Some quests have a penalty, which is taken off your balance.
  4. Earned Robux raise your level. Caris grows another fox tail every two levels, and Caius's gas grows stronger.
  5. When you want your Robux, ask Dad. He pays them out and records it here, so the balance is always right.

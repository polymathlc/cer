Before you join

  1. Charge your phone, tablet or computer, and sit somewhere quiet.
  2. Use headphones if you can, so the class does not echo.
  3. When the browser asks, press Allow for the camera and microphone.
  4. Tap Join live lesson, then find your name (or use your own link or PIN) and press Join.
  5. Keep the lesson screen open until the class ends — it counts towards your focus.

Help

I can't find my name

Tap I have a PIN and type the four numbers your teacher gave you. Still stuck? Tell your teacher, or WhatsApp us at +65 9645 0588. You can also join on Zoom from My Polymath.

It says the lesson has not started

That is fine: leave the page open. It lets you in by itself when your teacher opens the class.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Safari, up to date. On an iPhone or iPad, use Safari. If the video freezes, close other apps and come back to the lesson.

My teacher sent me my own link

Open it once on this device. Next time, this page remembers it and Join live lesson goes straight to “You are … — Join”.

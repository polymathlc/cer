🗼 Science Spire

An endless climb with no final floor. Win card battles, answer science questions for bonuses, and open booster packs to build your deck. Beat a boss every 12 floors, recover health, and keep climbing against stronger foes.

Your score is recorded as you clear rooms. Keep going until you fall, or choose End climb whenever you are ready. Continuing after a boss uses no extra play credit.

Play cards using energy. Attacks hurt foes, skills give block & utility, powers are permanent buffs. Block resets each turn. Watch the enemy intent above their head and plan ahead.

Buy Spell Packs in the app with points you earn from questions to permanently collect cards and build your own starting deck — your climb begins with whatever deck you forged.

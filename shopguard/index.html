      Setup

      Shop Guard runs on the same Firebase project, sign-in and Gemini setup as your CER app, so there is very little to set up.

      1. Make sure your shop data is private

        The “Check everything works” list shows whether signed-out visitors are refused. It should say Private.

        If it says “Not private yet”, the privacy rules haven't been published. In GitHub open polymathlc/cer, then Actions, then “Shop Guard rules”, then Run workflow, tick “apply”, and press Run workflow. Come back here after the green tick and press Check again.

      2. Turn the shop computer into the camera station

        On the computer with the webcam, open polymathlc.com.sg/shopguard in Chrome or Edge, sign in with Google, and choose “Camera station”. Allow the camera when the browser asks.

      3. Draw your zones

        In the Zones tab, drag boxes over the display cases, the counter and anywhere customers shouldn't go, then press Save zones.

      4. Get alerts on your phone

        Set up Telegram in Settings: create a bot with @BotFather, paste its token, message the bot once, press “Find my chat”, then Save settings and “Send a test message”.

      5. Keep the station running

        In Settings, under This device, turn on “Start guarding as soon as this page opens”. In the computer's power settings, set the screen and sleep to Never. Keep the Shop Guard tab in front.

      6. Put up a CCTV notice

        Place a clear sign at the entrance saying the shop is under video surveillance, as Singapore's PDPA expects when recording people. Keep the clips private and delete them when you no longer need them.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The diagnostic test is worth $100 and is free. There is nothing to pay and no obligation to sign up for classes.

At our Clementi centre, during the Master Class session you choose. Sessions run from 7 December 2026 onwards.

Can my child sit the test in a session for a different class?

Yes. You can choose any session. Tell us the subject and level the test should be for, and we prepare the right paper.

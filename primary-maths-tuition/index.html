From “I don’t know where to start” to a clear plan.

Many children can do the sums they have seen before but freeze at an unfamiliar problem. We teach them how to think through it: what is given, what is asked, which relationship connects them, and which strategy fits.

SEE

Model drawing

Bar models turn word problems about fractions, ratio and percentage into pictures that show the relationship at a glance.

CHOOSE

Heuristics

Look for a pattern, make a systematic list, guess and check, work backwards, before-and-after: children learn when each strategy works.

EXPLAIN

Clear working

Neat, step-by-step working earns method marks even when an answer slips, and makes checking easier.

What we cover at each level.

Lessons follow the Singapore primary Mathematics syllabus, with extra time on the topics that appear most in challenging problem sums.

P4

Building number sense

  • Whole numbers, factors and multiples
  • Fractions and decimals
  • Area and perimeter
  • Angles, symmetry, tables and line graphs
P5

The heart of problem sums

  • Fractions and decimals in word problems
  • Percentage, ratio and rate
  • Area of triangles, volume of cubes and cuboids
  • Angles and geometry
P6 · PSLE

Exam-ready

  • Algebra, circles and speed
  • Pie charts and volume
  • Multi-step PSLE problem sums

How a lesson works.

  1. Revisit the foundationsA short warm-up checks the skills the day’s topic depends on.
  2. Learn a strategyMr Chung models a problem step by step and names the heuristic, so children can recognise it next time.
  3. Try it, then stretch itChildren solve similar problems, then harder variations that need the same thinking.
  4. Mark and fixWork is marked in class, and mistakes are corrected while the method is fresh.

Questions parents often ask.

Which levels do you teach for Maths?

Primary 4 to Primary 6. Classes are grouped by level, and P6 classes focus on the PSLE.

My child is fine with sums but struggles with problem sums. Will this help?

Yes, that is what our Maths lessons focus on. We use model drawing, heuristics and thinking skills so children learn to recognise the structure of a problem and choose a strategy, instead of guessing which operation to use.

Where are the classes?

In person at our centre in Clementi, Singapore, and live online. Both follow the same curriculum with the same teacher, so families can choose what suits them.

Can my child try a lesson first?

Yes. We offer a paid trial lesson: a full 1 hour 45 minute class with the group your child would join. You can see the timetable and book a trial on the schedule page.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the level and the class. WhatsApp us on +65 9645 0588 or send an enquiry and we will reply with the current fees and timetable for your child’s level.

Do you also teach Science?

Yes. We teach Primary Science from P3 to P6 with the same teacher. See our Primary Science tuition page.
